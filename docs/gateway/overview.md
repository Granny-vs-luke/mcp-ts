The **MCP Gateway** is a secure utility that exposes your local Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers to the cloud. By creating a secure tunnel, it allows remote AI assistants to interact with tools on your machine (like your local filesystem or databases) as if they were local to the assistant.

### Key Benefits

- **Connectivity**: Connect local tools to ChatGPT, Claude, and other cloud-based MCP clients.
- **Security**: Securely tunnel traffic without opening ports or configuring complex firewalls.
- **Unified Hub**: Manage all your local-to-cloud connections through a single persistent bridge.
